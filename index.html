Fellowship Attendance

Upload the Zoom participant report for a session. The app checks every Fellow on the roster, adds up how long each one was in the room, and saves the results as a new tab in the roster Sheet.

1 Connect the roster Sheet

Paste the link to the Google Sheet that holds the Fellow roster, then sign in with a Google account that can edit it. The page remembers the link on this computer.

Advanced

2 Upload the Zoom report

In Zoom, open Reports, choose Usage, find the meeting, and export the participants as CSV. Then pick that file here.

Session details

The date is read from the report. Change the times or the allowance if this session was different.

A Fellow is marked absent when they miss more than the allowed minutes. Time before the start or after the end does not count.

3 Check the results
